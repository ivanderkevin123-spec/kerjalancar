# Competitor and Alternative-Solutions Research

Purpose: find out what owners in segments A and B use today, what it costs, and whether anything already covers lead → quotation → follow-up → DP/balance on a phone in Bahasa Indonesia at a micro-business price. The "alternative" that matters most is doing nothing: WhatsApp + memory.

Status legend: **[VERIFIED date source]** we fetched or read it · **[SECONDARY]** third-party claim, not confirmed · **[TODO]** needs manual check.

## 1. Categories to cover

1. Status quo: WhatsApp Business app (labels, quick replies, catalog, away/greeting messages, broadcast lists), Google Sheets / Excel, notebook, phone Notes, Google Calendar
2. Indonesian SMB invoicing and finance: Paper.id, Kledo, Mekari Jurnal, Accurate, Zahir, BukuWarung, Lummo (ex-BukuKas), bank-provided "buku kas" apps
3. Indonesian CRM / WhatsApp CRM: Mekari Qontak, Barantum, Sprint Asia, Qiscus, OneSender, Kommo (Indonesian site), Respond.io, Wati
4. Global lightweight CRMs owners might find: Zoho Bigin, HubSpot free, Pipedrive, Notion/Trello templates
5. Vertical tools, segment A: Aplikasi 101 (estimates/RAB), Excel RAB templates sold on marketplaces, any "aplikasi kontraktor" on Play Store
6. Vertical tools, segment B: Bridestory Pro (vendor side), Weddingku, WO planning apps on Play Store, Google Forms + Sheets setups shared in WO communities
7. Vertical tools, segment C (for reference only): Bengkelbaik, Kasmini, other "aplikasi bengkel" on Play Store
8. Quotation/invoice generators: Canva templates, "invoice generator" apps on Play Store, Paper.id free tier

## 2. Fields to capture for each tool

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Target customer and size | Is it aimed at 1–10 person service businesses? |
| Price (IDR/month, per user or per workspace, minimum users, annual lock-in) | Sets our ceiling and floor |
| Free tier limits | Free is our real competitor |
| Lead pipeline | Yes / no / how deep |
| Quotation with line items, validity, share link or PDF | Core of our product |
| DP / installment / balance tracking | Core of our product |
| Follow-up tasks and reminders | Core of our product |
| WhatsApp: click-to-chat, API, templates | Our WA-first angle |
| Mobile UX (Android app or responsive web), works offline or on weak data | Owners are on site |
| Bahasa Indonesia UI, IDR formatting | Non-negotiable for our users |
| Onboarding time to first useful action | Benchmark for our 2-minute target |
| Adoption signals: Play Store installs and rating, review themes | Proxy for traction and complaints |
| Gaps we can exploit | Feeds positioning |

What this already tells us: the affordable tools are invoice-first (Paper.id) or accounting-first (Kledo); the WhatsApp-first CRMs are priced for teams of five or more (Qontak) or per-user in USD (Kommo, Bigin). Nothing found yet combines lead pipeline + quotation link + DP/balance + follow-up reminders at a micro-business price with Indonesian UI. This gap is a fact about our search, not proof of demand.

## 4. Manual verification checklist (do before Gate G1)

Must verify:

1. [ ] Install WhatsApp Business app; document exactly what labels, quick replies, catalog, and broadcast lists do today, with screenshots. This is the real competitor.
2. [ ] Paper.id free account: does it offer quotations (penawaran) and conversion to invoice? Time to first invoice on a phone?
3. [ ] Kledo official pricing page and whether its trial exposes quotations and receivables in a way a contractor would use.
4. [ ] Kommo Indonesian pricing in IDR and whether WhatsApp works there via the free click-to-chat or requires the paid API.
5. [ ] Zoho Bigin free plan on an Android phone: Indonesian UI available? IDR formatting?
6. [ ] Play Store searches: "aplikasi penawaran", "aplikasi RAB", "aplikasi kontraktor", "aplikasi wedding organizer", "aplikasi invoice". Record top 5 per query with installs, rating, and the three most common complaints in reviews.
7. [ ] Bridestory Pro: sign up as a vendor or ask a B interviewee to show the dashboard. Does it track DP/balance? Does it work for leads that come from Instagram rather than Bridestory?
8. [ ] Aplikasi 101 free tier on a phone: can a contractor produce a shareable quotation in under 5 minutes?
9. [ ] Tokopedia/Shopee: search "template penawaran Excel", "template RAB", "template invoice". Sellers and sales counts are a cheap demand signal.
10. [ ] Barantum and Sprint Asia pricing pages (likely enterprise; confirm and move on).
11. [ ] Facebook groups for renovasi/interior and WO vendors: read the last 30 days for posts about lost customers, unpaid balances, and tooling. Save 10 verbatim posts (anonymized).
12. [ ] Ask every interviewee what they pay for today (hosting, Canva, ads, any app) to calibrate price tolerance.

Nice to have:

13. [ ] HubSpot free CRM and Pipedrive: confirm they are English-first and per-user USD; note them as the "tried it, too heavy" story if interviewees mention them.
14. [ ] Notion/Trello templates circulating in Indonesian WO communities.
15. [ ] Indonesian data-protection law (UU PDP No. 27/2022) obligations for storing customers' names and phone numbers on behalf of a business, and cross-border hosting implications. Needs a credible source before Phase D.
